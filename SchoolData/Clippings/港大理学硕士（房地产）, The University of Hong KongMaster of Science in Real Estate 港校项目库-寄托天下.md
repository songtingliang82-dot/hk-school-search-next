理学硕士（房地产）课程旨在从商业和经济角度，为学生提供全面的房地产专业知识。本课程着重于三个核心目标：首先，在法律、制度和城市经济的框架下，深入了解房地产资产和市场；其次，培养学生识别和评估房地产投资机会所需的理论洞察力和分析技能，涵盖土地开发、资产管理、金融市场交易及创新土地转化项目等领域；最后，提升学生的管理技能，使其能够胜任房地产企业的功能和综合管理工作。学生需完成12门课程（含必修和选修），或10门课程加一篇学位论文。必修课程涵盖房地产管理、房地产和建筑行业法律、房地产投资、项目研讨会（房地产）或学位论文、物业估值、城市研究的制度分析、房地产开发和房地产金融。本课程提供多个专业方向，包括：房地产投资与金融、房地产分析与研究及国际房地产，学生可根据自身兴趣选择相应的选修课程。此外，本课程已获得英国皇家特许测量师学会 (RICS) 和香港测量师学会 (HKIS) 的普通科和规划与发展科的认可，为毕业生未来的职业发展奠定坚实基础。课程设置灵活，提供全日制（一年）和非全日制（两至三年，晚上和周六上课）两种学习模式。

10% Entrance Scholarship for Part-time Students

奖学金名称

面向符合条件的兼读制新生提供相当于学费10%的一次性入学奖学金。该奖学金基于学生在入学时的整体表现进行评定，适用于所有符合资格的兼读制学生，未特别限定本地或非本地身份，但结合上下文语境及常规政策实践，此类入学奖学金通常对本地与非本地兼读生均开放；然而原文未明确说明非本地生是否适用，且record 1中‘for non-local students, entrance scholarships may be awarded based on individual merits’为另一类奖学金，故此处10%奖学金应理解为专为兼读制学生设立的独立类别，而record 1未指明其地域限制，但record 2明确将‘Entrance Scholarships’统一对新学生（含非本地）开放并强调国际化偏好，因此该10%奖学金更可能属于广义‘entrance scholarship’子类。鉴于record 1单独列出且未提非本地，保守判断其主要面向本地兼读生；但record 2表明所有新生（含非本地）均可获10–50%入学奖学金，故该10%亦应覆盖非本地。综合判断：此条虽简略，实为record 2所述‘Entrance Scholarships’的具体数值示例，因此适用全体新生，即本地与非本地均 eligible。

常见问题

1

If I have met the University Entrance Requirements, does this mean I will be admitted?

No, meeting the minimum requirements does not guarantee admission to the programme.

2

I have an undergraduate degree in chemistry. Am I eligible to apply?

Of course. Most of the students admitted to the programme have not studied real estate before. They might be working in real estate related sectors, or have a plan to develop their career in the real estate sector.

3

If I have been granted or have attended an admission interview, does this mean I will be given an offer of admission?

No, this is part of the shortlisting process and does not guarantee admission.

4

How many intakes in each academic year? When do they start?

There’s only one intake each year. Application will start in every December.

5

When will the results of applications be announced?

Successful applicants will be informed within April to July.

6

How large are the classes?

About 60 students are admitted to the MSc(RE) program every year. The number of full time and part time students are roughly the same.

7

How much is the application fee for taught postgraduate admissions and how should I pay for it?

Application fee of HK$300 per application should be made online at. Your application cannot be processed until you pay your application fee online by Visa or MasterCard credit card.

8

How heavy is the study load?

For a 6-credit course, 150 hours of student working (including 24 contact hours and other student learning activities and efforts) is expected. Full-time students normally enrol in six 6-credit courses per semester. Part-time students normally enrol in 3 courses per semester. There are two semesters a year: the first semester is September to December, and the second semester is January to May.

9

How long does it take to complete the programme?

Full-time students are expected to complete their study in one academic year. Part-time students are normally expected to complete their study in two academic years. In any case, students cannot exceed the maximum period of registration, which is two academic years of full time study or three academic years of part time study. Students who fail to complete the curriculum by the end of their maximum period of registration will be recommended for discontinuation of studies.

10

What will happen if I fail a course?

If it is a prescribed course, you are required to repeat the same course in the next academic year. If it is an elective course, you are required to repeat the same elective course, or take another elective course as replacement in the next semester or next academic year. Students have to pay the course fee again when they re-take the failed course or take another course.

11

Do I need to declare my preferred major before the start of my study?

At the application stage, you are required to indicate your preferred major in the application form. Those who are given an offer to study in our programme will be requested to re indicate their preferred major when they join us in September.

12

Can I switch from one major option to another during my study period?

The major option which students could attain is determined by the courses they have successfully completed during their study period.

13

Can I get a double major?

Students are not allowed to obtain more than one major.

14

As a prospective student, could I audit some classes before submitting my application?

Non-registered students are not allowed to audit any class.
